# v1.6.35 — PTT release confirmed from the radio

v1.6.33 began checking a key-on against the radio's own status. This release does
the same when PTT is released, which for USB-connected radios still relied on the
CAT daemon's acknowledgement alone: a radio could be left transmitting while the
app showed receive. It was tested against the Hamlib version the app bundles and a
simulated radio. **It has not been run on a radio or on an Android device.**

Install the signed **`app-release.apk`** attached to this release.

## English

**PTT release**

- After the CAT daemon acknowledges a release, the app now reads the radio's own
  transmit status 0.6 s later. If the radio still reports transmit, the release
  is sent again, up to twice.
- Why: for Kenwood-protocol radios (QRP Labs QMX/QDX, Kenwood TS-480/TS-590,
  Elecraft K3/KX2/KX3, Lab599 TX-500 and others) the bundled Hamlib 4.5.5 sends
  `RX;`, waits 0.35 s and reports success without reading anything back. If
  `RX;` did not reach the radio, the app showed receive while the radio kept
  transmitting, with no error on screen.
- This happens in the background. Receive resumes as before, and the next over
  can be keyed without waiting for it. Keying again ends the check, and a
  repeated release is never sent once PTT has been keyed again.
- If the radio still reports transmit after both repeats, or reported transmit
  and then could not be asked again, the existing red notice "PTT was not
  confirmed over CAT" appears and the Rig tab shows "PTT OFF not confirmed: the
  rig still reports transmit". As with any unconfirmed release, TX is refused
  and the app keeps sending the release, until the radio reports receive or you
  disconnect the rig. The daemon's acknowledgement alone no longer ends this.
- Only a radio that has reported "transmit" after a key-on earlier in the same
  connection is asked at all, as in v1.6.33: some CAT emulations report receive
  all the time.
- The notice, and the refusal to transmit, are for a radio whose status has also
  been seen to go back to "receive" after an earlier release. A status that
  stays on transmit, or follows the radio by several seconds, would otherwise
  end every over that way. Until then a release is still repeated, but if the
  status stays on transmit nothing is shown, and from the next over on the app
  only reads it.
- A status that cannot be read changes nothing: the daemon's acknowledgement
  stands, as before.
- Icom Wi-Fi connections are unchanged; their release was already read back.

**Diagnostics**

- The CAT log shows every check (`confirmReleased: rig reports …`) and every
  repeated release (`released again`).

## 繁體中文

**放開 PTT**

- CAT 服務程式確認放開 PTT 之後，app 會在 0.6 秒後讀取電台自己的收發狀態；如果
  仍是發射，就重送放開指令，最多兩次。
- 原因：內建的 Hamlib 4.5.5 對 Kenwood 協定的電台（QRP Labs QMX/QDX、Kenwood
  TS-480／TS-590、Elecraft K3／KX2／KX3、Lab599 TX-500 等）送出 `RX;` 後等 0.35 秒
  就回報成功，完全不讀回任何東西。`RX;` 沒有送達電台時，app 顯示接收，電台卻繼續
  發射，畫面沒有任何錯誤。
- 這項檢查在背景進行。接收照舊立刻恢復，下一次發射也不必等它；再次按下發射會
  結束這次檢查，而且重新 key-on 之後不會再送出重送的放開指令。
- 兩次重送後仍是發射時，或回報發射之後就再也讀不到狀態時，畫面會出現原有的紅字
  「未收到 CAT 的 PTT 確認」，Rig 分頁顯示「PTT OFF not confirmed: the rig still
  reports transmit」。和其他未確認的放開一樣，app 會拒絕新的發射並持續重送放開
  指令，直到電台回報接收，或你中斷電台連線為止。這時光有 CAT 服務程式的確認，
  不會解除這個狀態。
- 和 v1.6.33 一樣，只有在這次連線中 key-on 之後曾回報過「發射」的電台才會被
  詢問，因為有些 CAT 模擬韌體永遠回報接收。
- 紅字與拒絕發射，只用在「放開之後曾被看到回到『接收』」的電台上。否則，狀態
  一直停在發射、或比電台實際狀態慢好幾秒的機型，每次發射結束都會落入這個狀態。
  在那之前仍會重送放開指令；如果重送後狀態還是發射，不顯示任何訊息，從下一次
  發射起只讀取狀態、不再重送。
- 狀態讀不到時不做任何改變：照舊以 CAT 服務程式的確認為準。
- Icom Wi-Fi 連線沒有變動，它的放開本來就會讀回確認。

**診斷**

- CAT 紀錄會列出每次檢查（`confirmReleased: rig reports …`）與每次重送的放開
  指令（`released again`）。

## 日本語

**PTT の解除**

- CAT デーモンが PTT の解除に応答したあと、0.6 秒後に無線機自身の送受信状態を
  読み取ります。送信のままなら解除コマンドを再送します（最大 2 回）。
- 理由：同梱の Hamlib 4.5.5 は、Kenwood 系プロトコルの無線機（QRP Labs QMX/QDX、
  Kenwood TS-480／TS-590、Elecraft K3／KX2／KX3、Lab599 TX-500 など）に `RX;` を
  送ると、0.35 秒待つだけで何も読み返さずに成功とします。`RX;` が無線機に届かな
  かった場合、アプリは受信表示なのに無線機は送信を続け、画面にエラーも出ません
  でした。
- この確認はバックグラウンドで行います。受信はこれまでどおりすぐ再開し、次の送信
  も確認を待たずに始められます。もう一度送信を始めると確認は打ち切られ、key-on
  のあとに解除の再送が出ることはありません。
- 2 回再送しても送信のままの場合、または送信と報告したあと状態を読めなくなった
  場合は、従来の赤字「CAT の PTT 応答を確認できません」を表示し、Rig タブには
  「PTT OFF not confirmed: the rig still reports transmit」と表示します。ほかの
  未確認の解除と同じく、新しい送信は受け付けず、無線機が受信を報告するか接続を
  切るまで解除コマンドを送り続けます。この状態は CAT デーモンの応答だけでは解除
  されません。
- v1.6.33 と同じく、確認の対象は、その接続中に key-on のあと一度「送信」を報告
  した無線機だけです。常に受信と答える CAT エミュレーションがあるためです。
- 赤字の表示と送信の拒否は、解除のあとに状態が「受信」へ戻るのを一度確認できた
  無線機に限ります。状態が送信のまま変わらない機種や、実際より数秒遅れて変わる
  機種では、送信のたびにこの状態になってしまうためです。それまでも解除の再送は
  行いますが、再送しても送信のままなら何も表示せず、次の送信からは状態を読むだけ
  にします。
- 状態を読み取れない場合は何も変わりません。これまでどおり CAT デーモンの応答を
  そのまま採用します。
- Icom Wi-Fi 接続は変更ありません。解除は以前から読み返して確認しています。

**診断**

- CAT ログに、確認のたびの結果（`confirmReleased: rig reports …`）と、解除を再送
  したこと（`released again`）を記録します。

## Validation and limits

- **Not verified on hardware.** No Android device or radio was connected, and the
  simulated radio follows the QMX CAT manual, not a QMX. Not observed: whether
  `RX;` is ever lost on a real USB link, how soon a real radio's status returns
  to receive after `RX;`, and how any radio other than the simulated one reports
  its status around a release.
- Measured on the real daemon (`rigctld` of Ubuntu 24.04's `libhamlib-utils`
  4.5.5-3.2build2; it reports Hamlib 4.5.5, SHA 6eecd3, as the bundled rigctld
  does) with that simulated radio: `set_ptt 0` was acknowledged after 352 ms
  whether `RX;` reached the radio, was lost, or was refused with `?;`. Status
  requests 50, 200, 400 and 480 ms after the acknowledgement were answered
  "receive" without the radio being asked; at 520 ms the radio was asked.
- With `RADE_TEST_RIGCTLD=/usr/bin/rigctld` (that daemon): 224 tests passed,
  none skipped; v1.6.33 had 185. Six of the 39 new ones run the app's
  `RigController` against the real daemon and the simulated radio on a PTY:
  Hamlib acknowledges a release the radio never received, and one it refused,
  and the check then releases the radio; a status request right after a release
  is answered by Hamlib alone; a radio that stays in transmit is reported, and
  cleared once its own status reads receive; with the app's poller running, six
  releases that reached the radio were confirmed without a repeat and a lost
  one was repeated once.
- Without it: 204 passed, 20 skipped (the 14 skipped before, and those six).
- The other 33 new tests use a scripted rigctld. A missed release is
  repeated and confirmed, also with the poller running. A radio known to return
  to receive that stays in transmit is reported and left alone; it stays
  reported through further acknowledgements, polls, an unreadable status and a
  reconnect to the same daemon, and is cleared by a status that reads receive or
  by a key-on. Once such a radio has reported transmit, a repeat that goes
  unanswered or cannot be sent, a status that can no longer be read and an
  abandoned check all leave the release unconfirmed. None of this applies to a
  status never seen to return to receive, which is repeated after its first
  release only; "receive" after an over not seen transmitting does not count as
  returning. An untrusted or unreadable status, an unacknowledged release and a
  network release lead to no action. Nothing is sent once a key-on has begun, whether it
  begins while the check waits, while its status request is in flight, or just
  before a repeat is written; a key-on asked for while a repeat is in flight is
  written behind it. A cancelled check sends nothing more. The unkey retries
  take an acknowledgement only where the status does not contradict it, and not
  at all once a key-on has overtaken the release.
- Taking out each of 34 protections in turn makes the matching tests fail.
- A stress harness that is not part of the repository raced a new key-on, its
  own status check and cancellation against the release check 106,799 times
  with random timing, a third of them with the poller running. 53,616 repeats
  were written; 6,013 times the check was about to write one when the key-on
  or the cancellation arrived, and did not. The daemon never read an OFF after
  the key-on. With the claim deliberately moved to just before the write
  instead of during it, the same harness failed within 1,500 iterations in each
  of its three cases.
- An independent review of the first version found no way for a repeated
  release to follow a key-on. It found these defects, all fixed in this version:
  a release sent while a radio was held as still transmitting (Stop, or the TX
  button pressed and let go) was taken on the daemon's acknowledgement and
  lifted the refusal to transmit; after a radio had reported transmit, a check
  that then learned nothing more ended as if the release had worked; unkey
  retries left running by two overlapping releases could, when the next over
  began inside the new 0.6 s wait, clear the app's record that it had keyed the
  radio, so that this over's release was skipped; "receive" after an over never
  seen transmitting counted as the status returning; the displayed state could
  be written outside its lock. Of the fix for the overlapping releases, the
  part in `RigController` is tested and the part in the ViewModel is not.
- Two releases can still overlap, as before this version (Stop while the TX
  button is held, then letting go). What that could lead to is closed; the
  overlap itself is not.
- Arm64 Debug APK built with NDK 28.2; version code `10635`, version name
  `1.6.35-ptt-release-check`. The release workflow verifies the signed APK
  against the existing certificate.
- `TransceiverViewModel` has no unit tests. What it adds was checked by reading
  and by that review only: it starts the check behind the release, cancels it
  when the next over or a newer release begins or the rig is disconnected,
  enters the unconfirmed state on a verdict, and ends unkey retries when a new
  over begins. The decision those retries make is in `RigController` and is
  tested.
- The first over of a connection is not reported. Until the status has been
  seen to go from transmit back to receive across a release, a radio that
  stays in transmit gets the two repeats and no notice. Connecting again starts
  this over.
- After a manual reconnect, what was learned about the radio is forgotten. An
  unconfirmed release still pending from before is then ended by the daemon's
  acknowledgement, as before this version.
- The check cannot tell "did not unkey" from "keyed again by something else".
  If the radio is transmitting 0.6 s after the release for another reason (VOX
  still holding, the microphone's PTT, a transmit button on the radio, another
  program on the same daemon), the app sends the release up to twice more,
  which can end a transmission started at the radio in that moment. If the
  radio stays in transmit, the notice is shown until it reports receive.
- While the notice is shown, TX is refused. A radio whose status stuck on
  transmit after it had returned to receive earlier in the connection would
  stay that way until the rig is disconnected and connected again.
- A radio whose status cannot be read gets no check. That includes a USB link
  that passes nothing at all: the status request fails with it.
- A radio whose Hamlib backend cannot read PTT reports whatever was last
  commanded, so its release is never contradicted.
- Each over costs one more status request, 0.6 s after the release. A key-on
  pressed while that request or a repeat is with the daemon waits for it:
  about 0.35 s for a repeat (Kenwood protocol), longer if the radio does not
  answer the request. Where an earlier release was not acknowledged, the retry
  that is acknowledged now takes 0.6 s longer to clear the notice.
