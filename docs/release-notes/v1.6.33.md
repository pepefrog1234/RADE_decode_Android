# v1.6.33 — RADE V2 tuning hint

Since v1.6.32 the experimental V2 receiver refuses to sync on a signal that is
about 31–94 Hz off frequency instead of decoding garbled speech — but the app only
showed SEARCH, with no hint why. The receiver screen now says which way to tune,
using the tuning indicator added to the modem library (`rade_mistune()`), and
shows the V2 sound-card clock offset. V1, the default waveform, is unchanged.

Install the signed **`app-release.apk`** attached to this release.

## English

**V2 (experimental — Settings → RADE Mode)**

- Tuning hint. When a V2 signal is too far off frequency to sync on, an amber
  **TUNE UP** or **TUNE DOWN** with an arrow appears under the sync indicator,
  together with the size of the step: about 60 Hz. It shows 4–5 s after the
  signal starts and goes away once the signal is tuned into range (the receiver
  syncs), or about 2 s after the signal ends.
- The arrow follows the radio's sideband. With CAT connected the app reads it from
  the rig's mode (USB/LSB and their data modes); a directly connected Hermes-Lite 2
  is always USB. Otherwise, or in a mode with no clear sideband, the app cannot
  know which way the dial has to go, so the hint reads **SIGNAL TOO HIGH IN
  FREQUENCY** or **SIGNAL TOO LOW IN FREQUENCY** and names the correction for both
  sidebands (too high: up on USB, down on LSB).
- Sound-card clock offset. Below the SNR and FREQ cards, V2 shows the combined
  TX/RX sound-card clock offset in ppm that the receiver measures while in sync.
  The reading starts again with every over and climbs slowly, so it is marked
  **(preliminary)** until the receiver has been in sync for 45 s. It turns amber
  beyond ±125 ppm, which usually means one station's audio hardware is out of
  spec.
- V1 shows neither and behaves as before.

**Under the hood**

- The modem's first reading of the direction rests on about one second of signal,
  and on a fading channel it can point the wrong way for a moment. The app shows a
  direction only after the modem has held it for 3 s, and never while in sync or
  while no audio is reaching the modem.
- A CAT connection no longer keeps the mode of the previous one: after a reconnect
  the Rig tab shows no mode, and the hint no arrow, until the mode has been read
  again.
- New host check `tools/radecheck/tunecheck`: generates V2 signals, detunes or
  resamples them, and runs them through the modem and the app's own indicator
  code. Run it with `loopback` after every modem update — the hint's direction
  depends on sign conventions inside the modem.
- The periodic RX log lines (USB and network audio) also carry the tuning
  indicator and clock offset, for device reports.

## 繁體中文

**V2（實驗性，設定 → RADE 模式）**

- 調諧提示：V2 訊號頻率偏差太大、無法同步時，同步指示下方會出現琥珀色的
  **頻率往上調** 或 **頻率往下調** 與箭頭，並註明要調多少：約 60 Hz。提示在訊號
  出現約 4–5 秒後顯示；調到範圍內（完成同步）後、或訊號結束約 2 秒後消失。
- 箭頭方向依電台的邊帶決定。連接 CAT 時取自電台模式（USB／LSB 及其數據模式），
  直接連線的 Hermes-Lite 2 固定為 USB。其他情況、或模式無法判斷邊帶時，app 無從
  得知該往哪邊調，改顯示 **訊號頻率偏高** 或 **訊號頻率偏低**，並列出兩種邊帶
  各自的調法（偏高：USB 往上調、LSB 往下調）。
- 音效卡時脈偏差：V2 模式下，訊噪比／頻偏下方會顯示接收端在同步期間量到的收發
  兩端音效卡時脈偏差（ppm）。讀數在每次發話開始時歸零後慢慢上升，連續同步滿
  45 秒前會標示 **（暫定）**。超過 ±125 ppm 時轉為琥珀色，通常代表其中一方的
  音訊硬體不合規格。
- V1 兩者都不顯示，行為與先前相同。

**內部變更**

- 數據機最初的方向判斷只根據約一秒的訊號，在衰落通道上可能短暫指向相反方向。
  app 會等數據機連續 3 秒回報同一方向後才顯示；同步期間、或沒有音訊送進數據機時
  一律不顯示。
- CAT 重新連線後不再沿用上一次連線的模式：重新讀到模式之前，電台分頁不顯示
  模式，提示也不顯示箭頭。
- 新增主機端檢查 `tools/radecheck/tunecheck`：產生 V2 訊號後加上頻率偏移或重新
  取樣，再交給數據機與 app 自己的提示判斷程式。提示的方向取決於數據機內部的
  正負號約定，每次更新數據機後請與 `loopback` 一併執行。
- 定期輸出的 RX 記錄（USB 與網路音訊）新增調諧指示與時脈偏差，方便回報裝置
  問題。

## 日本語

**V2（実験的 — 設定 → RADE モード）**

- チューニング表示：V2 信号の周波数ずれが大きく同期できないとき、同期表示の下に
  矢印付きの **周波数を上げる** または **周波数を下げる** をアンバー色で表示し、
  動かす量（約 60 Hz）も示します。信号が出てから 4〜5 秒で現れ、範囲内に合わせて
  同期するか、信号が終わって約 2 秒たつと消えます。
- 矢印の向きは無線機のサイドバンドで決まります。CAT 接続時は無線機のモード
  （USB／LSB とそのデータモード）から判断し、直接接続した Hermes-Lite 2 は常に
  USB です。それ以外の場合やサイドバンドが決まらないモードでは、ダイヤルを
  どちらへ回すべきかアプリには分からないため、**信号の周波数が高すぎます**
  または **信号の周波数が低すぎます** と表示し、サイドバンドごとの合わせ方を
  示します（高すぎる場合：USB は上げる、LSB は下げる）。
- サウンドカードのクロックずれ：V2 では SNR／周波数偏差の下に、受信機が同期中に
  測定した送受信サウンドカードのクロックずれ（ppm）を表示します。値は送信ごとに
  0 から始まってゆっくり上がるため、同期が 45 秒続くまでは **（暫定）** と表示
  します。±125 ppm を超えるとアンバー色になり、多くの場合どちらかの局の
  オーディオ機器が規格外です。
- V1 ではどちらも表示されず、動作は従来どおりです。

**内部の変更**

- モデムが最初に出す向きは約 1 秒分の信号だけに基づいており、フェージングのある
  伝搬では一瞬逆を指すことがあります。アプリはモデムが同じ向きを 3 秒間続けて
  報告してから表示し、同期中やモデムに音声が届いていない間は表示しません。
- CAT の再接続後、前回の接続のモードを引き継がなくなりました。モードを読み直す
  まで、リグ画面にモードは表示されず、チューニング表示にも矢印は出ません。
- ホスト用チェック `tools/radecheck/tunecheck` を追加しました。V2 信号を生成して
  周波数をずらす、またはリサンプルし、モデムとアプリ自身の表示判定コードに
  通します。表示の向きはモデム内部の符号の取り決めに依存するため、モデム更新の
  たびに `loopback` と合わせて実行してください。
- 定期的な RX ログ（USB とネットワーク音声）にチューニング表示とクロックずれを
  追加しました（端末からの報告用）。

## Validation and limits

- `tools/radecheck/tunecheck` on an x86-64 host under AddressSanitizer, through the
  app's int16 conversion and its indicator code (`tune_indicator.h`): a V2 signal
  45 Hz too high gives +1 and 45 Hz too low gives −1, in both cases without the
  receiver ever syncing. The modem reports the direction 1.3 s after the signal
  starts, the app shows it at 4.3 s and clears it 1.8 s after the signal ends. A
  2 s burst shows nothing. On frequency, on noise alone and on V1 there is no
  indication. After one 60 Hz step in the indicated direction the receiver syncs
  0.3 s later and nothing is shown from then on. The same check linked against
  the arm64 modem libraries the debug APK is built from, run under QEMU, gives
  identical results. `loopback` still passes.
- The app's own `audio_engine.cpp`, compiled unmodified for the host against a
  stand-in for the Oboe audio API and fed through its network-RX entry point in
  20 ms packets at 48 kHz and 16 kHz, reports the same through the getters the UI
  polls, and additionally nothing while the audio feed stalls for more than a
  second and nothing right after the engine restarts on a new modem. This was a
  one-off harness and is not in the repository.

The remaining figures come from one-off host runs with the same modem build, not
from `tunecheck`:

- Fading. 320 simulated 20 s overs, 35–70 Hz off, through two-path Rayleigh
  fading (0.5–2 ms delay, 0.1–1 Hz Doppler spread), half of them with noise at
  10 dB SNR: `rade_mistune()` itself pointed the wrong way in 12 overs, in 10 of
  them as its very first reading, for 0.2–2.6 s at a time; with the 3 s
  confirmation the app showed a wrong direction in none. The correct hint
  appeared after 4.9 s on average, 17.6 s at the latest, and not at all in 5
  overs. On-frequency signals on the same kinds of channel synced and never
  showed a hint.
- A deep selective fade that does not move is not covered by the confirmation:
  with a static second path of 70–100 % at 0.5–2 ms, 12 of 96 off-frequency
  signals (all at 0.5–1 ms) showed the wrong direction for as long as they
  lasted, and 15 showed none.
- Without fading, with white noise from +20 dB down to −4 dB SNR (3 kHz), a
  signal ±45 Hz off always gave the correct direction. 60 s of noise alone gave
  no indication, and neither did V1 signals received in V2 mode (on frequency
  and up to 100 Hz off).
- Only an offset of about one carrier spacing is detected. In a sweep of a clean
  signal from −250 to +250 Hz: sync and no hint within ±31 Hz; no sync and the
  correct direction at 33–90 Hz and again at 160–200 Hz; but at 95–150 Hz and
  225–250 Hz the receiver synced on a wrong carrier, decoded garbage and showed
  no hint (upstream documents offsets of two or more carriers as not detected).
  The v1.6.32 note "more than ±31 Hz off frequency is no longer decoded as garbled
  speech" therefore holds up to about ±94 Hz.
- "About 60 Hz": one 60 Hz step in the indicated direction brought every tested
  offset from 33 to 90 Hz, on either side, into sync within 0.3 s. At 93 Hz a
  second small step was needed.
- The direction lags after an overshoot. The modem accumulates its evidence for as
  long as it keeps refusing to sync, so a signal that jumps from 45 Hz too low to
  45 Hz too high is still reported as too low for a while: 3 s after 4 s on the
  wrong side, 16 s after 20 s on the wrong side, followed by a period with no
  hint at all. A single 60 Hz step does not cross to the other side.
- The sideband is read from CAT every few seconds; a rig switched between USB and
  LSB by hand can show the previous direction until the next read. With a
  Hermes-Lite 2 connected the arrow assumes its audio is what is being decoded.
- Clock offset, Rx sound card 100 ppm fast or slow: +75 / −75 ppm after 30 s,
  +98 ppm after 45 s, +104 ppm after 60 s. At ±180 ppm the reading passes
  ±125 ppm after about 28 s, so an out-of-spec card is only flagged in overs
  longer than that.

Build and tests:

- JVM unit tests: 167 passed (20 new, covering the hint's sideband mapping, the
  conditions under which it is shown, the clock offset states and the CAT mode
  after a reconnect); 9 optional real-Hamlib integration tests skipped because
  `RADE_TEST_RIGCTLD` was not configured.
- UI: a new instrumented test (`TransceiverTuneHintTest`) and the extended small-
  screen layout test were not run on a device. Both pass on the host JVM under
  Robolectric, where the receiver screen was also rendered and reviewed in all
  four languages, at 360 dp and at 320×480 dp with a 1.3× font. Renders of V1
  from v1.6.32 and from this version are pixel-identical (16 pairs), also when
  the new code is fed a tuning indication and a clock offset in V1 mode.
- Arm64 Debug APK built with NDK 28.2; version code `10633`, version name
  `1.6.33-v2-tuning-hint`. The release workflow verifies the signed APK against
  the existing certificate.
- No Android device or radio was connected: the hint has not been seen on the air,
  and the mode read back from real rigs over CAT was not exercised.
