# v1.6.33 — RADE V2 tuning hint

Since v1.6.32 the experimental V2 receiver refuses to sync on a signal that is
about 31–94 Hz off frequency instead of decoding garbled speech — but the app only
showed SEARCH, with no hint why. The receiver screen now says which way to tune,
using the tuning indicator added to the modem library (`rade_mistune()`), and
shows the V2 sound-card clock offset. V1, the default waveform, is unchanged.

Install the signed **`app-release.apk`** attached to this release.

## English

**V2 (experimental — Settings → RADE Mode)**

- Tuning hint. When a V2 signal is too far off frequency to sync on, an amber
  **TUNE UP** or **TUNE DOWN** with an arrow appears under the sync indicator. It
  shows about 1.5 s after the signal starts and goes away once the signal is
  tuned into range (the receiver syncs), or about 2 s after the signal ends.
- The arrow follows the radio's sideband. With CAT connected the app reads it from
  the rig's mode (USB/LSB and their data modes); a directly connected Hermes-Lite 2
  is always USB. Without CAT, or in a mode with no clear sideband, the app cannot
  know which way the dial has to go, so the hint reads **SIGNAL TOO HIGH IN
  FREQUENCY** or **SIGNAL TOO LOW IN FREQUENCY** and names the correction for both
  sidebands (too high: tune up on USB, down on LSB).
- Sound-card clock offset. Below the SNR and FREQ cards, V2 shows the combined
  TX/RX sound-card clock offset in ppm that the receiver measures while in sync.
  It turns amber beyond ±125 ppm, which usually means one station's audio hardware
  is out of spec. The reading needs a long over: after 30 s it has covered about
  three quarters of the real offset.
- V1 shows neither and behaves as before.

**Under the hood**

- New host check `tools/radecheck/tunecheck`: generates V2 signals, detunes or
  resamples them, and reads the indicator and clock offset the way the app does.
  Run it with `loopback` after every modem update — the hint's direction depends
  on sign conventions inside the modem.
- The periodic RX log line also carries the tuning indicator and clock offset, for
  device reports.

## 繁體中文

**V2（實驗性，設定 → RADE 模式）**

- 調諧提示：V2 訊號頻率偏差太大、無法同步時，同步指示下方會出現琥珀色的
  **頻率往上調** 或 **頻率往下調** 與箭頭。提示約在訊號出現 1.5 秒後顯示；調到
  範圍內（完成同步）後、或訊號結束約 2 秒後消失。
- 箭頭方向依電台的邊帶決定。連接 CAT 時取自電台模式（USB／LSB 及其數據模式），
  直接連線的 Hermes-Lite 2 固定為 USB。沒有 CAT、或模式無法判斷邊帶時，app 無從
  得知該往哪邊調，改顯示 **訊號頻率偏高** 或 **訊號頻率偏低**，並列出兩種邊帶
  各自的調法（偏高：USB 往上調、LSB 往下調）。
- 聲卡時脈偏差：V2 同步期間，訊噪比／頻偏下方會顯示接收端量到的收發兩端聲卡
  時脈偏差（ppm）。超過 ±125 ppm 時轉為琥珀色，通常代表其中一方的音訊硬體不合
  規格。讀數需要較長的發話才會到位：30 秒時約為實際偏差的四分之三。
- V1 兩者都不顯示，行為與先前相同。

**內部變更**

- 新增主機端檢查 `tools/radecheck/tunecheck`：產生 V2 訊號後加上頻率偏移或重新
  取樣，再以 app 相同的方式讀取調諧指示與時脈偏差。提示的方向取決於數據機內部的
  正負號約定，每次更新數據機後請與 `loopback` 一併執行。
- 定期輸出的 RX 記錄新增調諧指示與時脈偏差，方便回報裝置問題。

## 日本語

**V2（実験的 — 設定 → RADE モード）**

- チューニング表示：V2 信号の周波数ずれが大きく同期できないとき、同期表示の下に
  矢印付きの **周波数を上げる** または **周波数を下げる** をアンバー色で表示
  します。信号が出てから約 1.5 秒で現れ、範囲内に合わせて同期するか、信号が
  終わって約 2 秒たつと消えます。
- 矢印の向きは無線機のサイドバンドで決まります。CAT 接続時は無線機のモード
  （USB／LSB とそのデータモード）から判断し、直接接続した Hermes-Lite 2 は常に
  USB です。CAT がない場合やサイドバンドが決まらないモードでは、ダイヤルを
  どちらへ回すべきかアプリには分からないため、**信号の周波数が高すぎます**
  または **信号の周波数が低すぎます** と表示し、サイドバンドごとの合わせ方を
  示します（高すぎる場合：USB は上げる、LSB は下げる）。
- サウンドカードのクロックずれ：V2 で同期している間、SNR／周波数偏差の下に、
  受信機が測定した送受信サウンドカードのクロックずれ（ppm）を表示します。
  ±125 ppm を超えるとアンバー色になり、多くの場合どちらかの局のオーディオ機器が
  規格外です。値が落ち着くには長い送信が必要で、30 秒で実際のずれの約 4 分の 3
  に達します。
- V1 ではどちらも表示されず、動作は従来どおりです。

**内部の変更**

- ホスト用チェック `tools/radecheck/tunecheck` を追加しました。V2 信号を生成して
  周波数をずらす、またはリサンプルし、アプリと同じ方法でチューニング表示と
  クロックずれを読み取ります。表示の向きはモデム内部の符号の取り決めに依存する
  ため、モデム更新のたびに `loopback` と合わせて実行してください。
- 定期的な RX ログにチューニング表示とクロックずれを追加しました（端末からの
  報告用）。

## Validation and limits

- `tools/radecheck/tunecheck` on an x86-64 host under AddressSanitizer, through the
  app's int16 conversion: a V2 signal 45 Hz too high gives `rade_mistune()` +1 and
  45 Hz too low gives −1, in both cases without the receiver ever syncing; the
  indication appears 1.3 s after the signal starts and clears 1.9 s after it ends.
  On frequency, on noise alone and on V1 there is no indication. A signal retuned
  into range syncs 0.3 s later and shows no indication from then on. The same
  check linked against the arm64 modem libraries the debug APK is built from, run
  under QEMU, gives identical results. `loopback` still passes.
- The app's own `audio_engine.cpp`, compiled unmodified for the host against a
  stand-in for the Oboe audio API and fed through its network-RX entry point in
  20 ms packets at 48 kHz and 16 kHz, reports the same through the getters the UI
  polls: +1 / −1 without ever reporting SYNC, nothing on frequency, on noise or
  on V1, nothing from the moment SYNC is reported, and nothing right after the
  engine restarts on a new modem. This was a one-off harness and is not in the
  repository.
- With white noise added, from +20 dB down to −4 dB SNR (3 kHz), a signal ±45 Hz
  off always showed the correct direction and never the opposite one, 1.3–1.5 s
  after it started. 60 s of noise alone gave no indication.
- Only an offset of about one carrier spacing is detected. In a host sweep of a
  clean signal from −250 to +250 Hz: sync and no hint within ±31 Hz; no sync and
  the correct direction at 33–90 Hz and again at 160–200 Hz; but at 95–150 Hz and
  225–250 Hz the receiver synced on a wrong carrier, decoded garbage and showed
  no hint (upstream documents offsets of two or more carriers as not detected).
  The v1.6.32 note "more than ±31 Hz off frequency is no longer decoded as garbled
  speech" therefore holds up to about ±94 Hz. The hint gives a direction, not an
  amount; tune in 10 Hz steps.
- The direction lags after an overshoot. The modem accumulates its evidence for as
  long as it keeps refusing to sync, so a signal that jumps from 45 Hz too low to
  45 Hz too high is still reported as too low for a while: 3 s after 4 s on the
  wrong side, 16 s after 20 s on the wrong side, followed by a period with no
  hint at all. Stepping in 10 Hz steps avoids crossing to the other side.
- The sideband is read from CAT every few seconds; a rig switched between USB and
  LSB by hand can show the previous direction until the next read. Without CAT
  the hint never names a dial direction.
- Clock offset, Rx sound card 100 ppm fast or slow: +75 / −75 ppm after 30 s,
  +98 ppm after 45 s, +104 ppm after 60 s. At ±180 ppm the reading passes
  ±125 ppm after about 28 s. Shorter overs under-read.
- JVM unit tests: 158 passed (11 new, covering the hint's sideband mapping and the
  conditions under which it is shown); 9 optional real-Hamlib integration tests
  skipped because `RADE_TEST_RIGCTLD` was not configured.
- UI: a new instrumented test (`TransceiverTuneHintTest`) and the extended small-
  screen layout test were not run on a device. Both pass on the host JVM under
  Robolectric, where the receiver screen was also rendered and reviewed in all
  four languages, at 360 dp and at 320×480 dp with a 1.3× font. Renders from
  v1.6.32 and from this version are pixel-identical in V1, and in V2 while there
  is no indication or clock estimate (16 pairs); V1 stays identical even when fed
  a tuning indication and a clock offset.
- Arm64 Debug APK built with NDK 28.2; version code `10633`, version name
  `1.6.33-v2-tuning-hint`. The release workflow verifies the signed APK against
  the existing certificate.
- No Android device or radio was connected: the hint has not been seen on the air,
  and the mode read back from real rigs over CAT was not exercised.
