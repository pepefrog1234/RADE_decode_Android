# v1.6.33 — PTT confirmed from the radio, and no longer held up by a failing S-meter

Responds to [issue #33](https://github.com/pepefrog1234/RADE_decode_Android/issues/33):
a QRP Labs QMX+ that intermittently stays in receive when TX is pressed. The report
came without logs, so its cause is not established. This release removes two ways
the app could produce exactly that symptom, both reproduced against the same Hamlib
version the app bundles, and makes the next CAT log show which case it is.

Install the signed **`app-release.apk`** attached to this release.

## English

**PTT**

- After the CAT daemon acknowledges a key-on, the app now reads the radio's own
  transmit status 0.6 s later. If the radio still reports receive, the key-on is
  sent again, up to twice.
- Why: for Kenwood-protocol radios (QRP Labs QMX/QDX, Kenwood TS-480/TS-590,
  Elecraft K3/KX2/KX3, Lab599 TX-500 and others) the bundled Hamlib 4.5.5 sends
  `TX;` followed by `ID;` and reports success as soon as `ID;` is answered —
  also when `TX;` itself was lost or rejected. The app then showed TX and sent
  audio while the radio stayed in receive, with no error on screen.
- If the radio still reports receive after both repeats, a red notice asks you to
  check that it is allowed to transmit (TX inhibit, SWR protection, band or mode).
  The over is not cut and PTT is not released for you.
- "Receive" is only held against a radio that has reported "transmit" earlier in
  the same connection, because some CAT emulations (uSDX firmware) report receive
  all the time. Until then the notice is not shown, and if repeating changes
  nothing the app stops repeating from the next over on.
- A repeat is never sent once PTT has been released.

**S-meter polling**

- When the radio fails an S-meter read, the app stops asking for it for 30 s,
  then for twice as long after each further failure, up to 5 minutes. It goes
  back to reading it on every poll only after five reads in a row have worked.
- Why: a failed S-meter read holds up every CAT command behind it, a key-on
  included. When the answer is one Hamlib 4.5.5 cannot parse, the CAT daemon
  closes the radio, waits a second and reopens it. With a radio whose S-meter
  answer has an unexpected length, key-ons pressed at eight points of the poll
  cycle reached the radio after 254, 834, 659, 508, 329, 161, 1 and 1135 ms;
  with this change all eight take 2 ms or less.
- While the S-meter is left alone, the Rig tab keeps showing its last reading.

**Diagnostics**

- The CAT log shows every status check (`confirmKeyed: rig reports …`), when
  S-meter polling is paused, and the bytes of any USB serial write that could
  not be sent.

## 繁體中文

**PTT**

- CAT 服務程式確認 key-on 之後，app 會在 0.6 秒後讀取電台自己的收發狀態；如果
  仍是接收，就重送 key-on，最多兩次。
- 原因：內建的 Hamlib 4.5.5 對 Kenwood 協定的電台（QRP Labs QMX/QDX、Kenwood
  TS-480／TS-590、Elecraft K3／KX2／KX3、Lab599 TX-500 等）送出 `TX;` 後會再送
  `ID;`，只要 `ID;` 有回應就回報成功，即使 `TX;` 本身遺失或被拒絕也一樣。這時 app
  顯示發射中並送出音訊，電台卻留在接收，畫面沒有任何錯誤。
- 兩次重送後仍是接收時，畫面會出現紅字，請你檢查電台是否允許發射（發射禁止、
  SWR 保護、波段或模式）。App 不會中止發射，也不會替你放開 PTT。
- 只有在這次連線中曾回報過「發射」的電台，它回報的「接收」才會被採信，因為有些
  CAT 模擬韌體（例如 uSDX）永遠回報接收。在那之前不會顯示紅字；如果重送後毫無
  變化，從下一次發射起就不再重送。
- 放開 PTT 之後不會再送出重送的指令。

**S 表輪詢**

- 電台的 S 表讀取一失敗，app 就暫停讀它 30 秒；之後每失敗一次暫停時間加倍，最長
  5 分鐘。連續五次讀取成功後才恢復每輪都讀。
- 原因：一次失敗的 S 表讀取會讓排在後面的所有 CAT 指令（包含 key-on）跟著等。回覆
  是 Hamlib 4.5.5 無法解析的格式時，CAT 服務程式會關閉電台、等一秒再重新開啟。
  以一台 S 表回覆長度不合預期的電台實測，在輪詢週期的八個位置按下 key-on，送達
  電台的時間分別是 254、834、659、508、329、161、1、1135 ms；修改後八次都在 2 ms
  以內。
- 暫停期間，Rig 分頁的 S 表停在最後一次讀到的值。

**診斷**

- CAT 紀錄會列出每次狀態檢查（`confirmKeyed: rig reports …`）、S 表輪詢何時暫停，
  以及 USB 序列埠沒能送出的位元組。

## 日本語

**PTT**

- CAT デーモンが key-on に応答したあと、0.6 秒後に無線機自身の送受信状態を
  読み取ります。受信のままなら key-on を再送します（最大 2 回）。
- 理由：同梱の Hamlib 4.5.5 は、Kenwood 系プロトコルの無線機（QRP Labs QMX/QDX、
  Kenwood TS-480／TS-590、Elecraft K3／KX2／KX3、Lab599 TX-500 など）に `TX;` に
  続けて `ID;` を送り、`ID;` に応答があれば成功とします。`TX;` が届かなかった
  場合や拒否された場合も成功になるため、アプリは送信表示のまま音声を送り、
  無線機は受信のまま、画面にエラーも出ませんでした。
- 2 回再送しても受信のままの場合は、無線機が送信できる状態か（送信禁止、SWR
  保護、バンドやモード）確認するよう赤字で表示します。送信は中断せず、PTT も
  自動では戻しません。
- 「受信」という応答を根拠にするのは、その接続中に一度「送信」を報告した無線機
  だけです。常に受信と答える CAT エミュレーション（uSDX のファームウェアなど）が
  あるためです。それまでは赤字を表示せず、再送しても変化がなければ次の送信から
  再送しません。
- PTT を離したあとに再送が出ることはありません。

**S メーターのポーリング**

- 無線機が S メーターの読み取りに失敗すると、30 秒間は読み取りを止めます。失敗が
  続くたびに休止時間を 2 倍にし、最長 5 分まで延ばします。5 回続けて成功すると、
  毎回読み取る動作に戻ります。
- 理由：S メーターの読み取りに失敗すると、その後ろに並んだ CAT コマンド（key-on を
  含む）がすべて待たされます。応答が Hamlib 4.5.5 の解釈できない形式の場合、CAT
  デーモンは無線機をいったん閉じ、1 秒待ってから開き直します。S メーターの応答の
  長さが想定と異なる無線機で、ポーリング周期の 8 か所で key-on を押したところ、
  無線機に届くまで 254、834、659、508、329、161、1、1135 ms かかりました。この変更
  後は 8 回とも 2 ms 以内です。
- 休止中、Rig タブの S メーターは最後に読めた値のままです。

**診断**

- CAT ログに、状態確認の結果（`confirmKeyed: rig reports …`）、S メーターの
  ポーリングを休止したこと、USB シリアルへ書き込めなかったバイト列を記録します。

## Validation and limits

- With `RADE_TEST_RIGCTLD=/usr/bin/rigctld` (Ubuntu 24.04's `libhamlib-utils`
  4.5.5-3.2build2; it reports Hamlib 4.5.5, SHA 6eecd3, as the bundled rigctld
  does): 185 tests passed, none skipped. That includes the 9 existing Icom
  integration tests and 5 new ones that run the app's `RigController` against
  the real daemon and a QMX-style radio on a PTY: Hamlib acknowledges a key-on
  the radio never received, and one it refused with `?;`, and the check then
  keys the radio; a key-on is not held up by an S-meter answer Hamlib rejects,
  every time or now and then.
- Without it: 171 passed, those 14 skipped.
- Twenty-four other tests are new. Eighteen use a scripted rigctld: a missed
  key-on is repeated and confirmed; a trusted radio that stays in receive is
  reported and not unkeyed; a radio that never reports transmit is repeated in
  its first over only; an unreadable status or an unanswered repeat leads to no
  action; nothing is sent after a cancellation or a release, including a release
  that begins after the status check has passed; one key-on is checked once, and
  none is sent when no key-on had been; a failed S-meter read is not repeated on
  the next poll. Six cover the pause schedule. Taking out each of these
  protections in turn makes the matching tests fail.
- An independent review of the first version found no way for a repeat to follow
  a release, and four defects around it: one of the two protections against that
  was not exercised by any test, a repeat could be sent when no key-on ever had
  been, the CAT log showed a repeat that was never sent, and a coroutine could
  leak. All four are fixed in this version; the last one, in the ViewModel, has
  no unit test.
- A stress harness that is not part of the repository raced a release against
  the check 42,000 times with random timing, and 3,000 more times with polling,
  cancellation and reconnects mixed in. No key-on was written after a release,
  and no key-on was repeated more than twice.
- Arm64 Debug APK built with NDK 28.2; version code `10633`, version name
  `1.6.33-ptt-status-check`. The release workflow verifies the signed APK against
  the existing certificate.
- No Android device or QMX was connected, and the simulated radio follows the
  QMX CAT manual, not a QMX. Whether this resolves issue #33 is unknown until the
  reporter tries it: what a QMX answers to `IF;` during SSB transmit and to
  `SM;`, and whether `TX;` is in fact being lost, have not been observed.
- The S-meter itself is not fixed: a radio whose answer Hamlib 4.5.5 rejects
  still shows no reading. It just no longer slows the other commands down.
- Frequency, PTT and mode are still read on every poll. A radio that fails
  those has the same one-second reopen; they are needed for control and are
  left to the existing CAT health handling.
- An over rescued by a repeat starts late: its first 0.7 s or so (about 1.5 s
  when both repeats are needed) is not transmitted.
- The check cannot tell "never keyed" from "keyed and dropped out again". If the
  radio leaves transmit by itself within the first second or two of an over (a
  protection trip, or TX cancelled at the radio), the app keys it again, up to
  twice.
- A radio that has never transmitted during the connection gets no notice,
  because "did not key" cannot yet be told from "does not report it".
- Releasing PTT is unchanged: for USB-connected radios it still relies on the
  daemon's acknowledgement alone.
- Corrects the v1.6.32 notes, which said the V2 end-of-over crash had not been
  seen on a device: it was reported from on-air use in issue #29.
