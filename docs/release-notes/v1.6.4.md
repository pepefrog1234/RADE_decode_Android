# v1.6.4 — Icom network: connect reliably over LTE / VPN

Fixes "Control handshake failed" when connecting to an Icom network rig (IC-705 / IC-7300mk2 "IC-705 Wi-Fi" mode) **over LTE through a VPN**, while the same setup worked on the local Wi-Fi.

## What was wrong

The app sent its initial UDP handshake (SYN) exactly twice, back-to-back, and waited a single 3-second window with **no retry**. On a cold LTE path the first packets are routinely lost while the link warms up (LTE idle→active transition, VPN tunnel state, ARP on the far LAN) — both packets died together and the app gave up, even though the path works moments later. Wi-Fi links are already warm, which is why it never showed there. Other Icom clients (kappanhang / wfview) keep resending the SYN until the radio answers; this app now does the same.

## Changes

- Handshake SYN: retried up to 8× at 1 s intervals; the follow-up packet up to 5×. Applies to all three streams (control 50001 / CI-V 50002 / audio 50003).
- Login reply window extended 3 s → 5 s for the first full round-trip on a cold path.

## Testing tip

The radio accepts only **one** control session at a time — fully close any other remote-control app (swipe it away, not just background it) before connecting with this app.
